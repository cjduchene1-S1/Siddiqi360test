Siddiqi 360 is a companion recovery app for patients of Dr. Ahmed Siddiqi at Texas Center for Joint Replacement. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Who this app is for

Siddiqi 360 is available only to patients of Texas Center for Joint Replacement with a scheduled or completed surgery. Access requires a code provided directly by our office.

What we collect

  • Access code and surgery date — entered by you at sign-in, to determine which stage of recovery content to show you.
  • Task and exercise checkmarks, pain level entries — used only to show your own progress back to you within the app.

We do not collect your name, contact information, medical record number, or any other identifying health information through this app.

Where your information is stored

  • Your progress data (checkmarks, pain logs, surgery date) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to it, and it is not transmitted to our office through this app.
  • Access codes are validated against a secure backend used solely to confirm a code is valid — this system stores only the codes themselves, never patient names or identifiers.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics that identify you personally.
  • We do not use your information for any purpose beyond operating the app's features described above.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may send you reminders about medications, exercises, or appointments based on your surgery date. These are generated and delivered on your device and are not monitored by our office in real time.

Your choices

  • You can sign out of the app at any time, which clears your locally stored data from that device.
  • You can use the in-app "Reset all progress" option to clear your checkmarks and pain logs without signing out.
  • You can decline notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adult patients undergoing joint replacement surgery and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.
