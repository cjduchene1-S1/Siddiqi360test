A note before surgery

The patients who do best are not the ones in the best shape going in. They are the ones who know what to expect at each stage.

Dr. Ahmed Siddiqi

Expectation is the strongest predictor of satisfaction after joint replacement. Patients who understand the normal course of recovery report less distress at the six-week mark, even when their measured function is identical to patients who were not prepared. Knowing that day four is usually the hardest day changes how you experience day four.
